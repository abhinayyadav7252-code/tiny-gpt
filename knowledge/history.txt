The Apollo 11 moon landing occurred in 1969. Neil Armstrong was the first man to walk on the moon, famously stating "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."
World War II was a global war that lasted from 1939 to 1945. It involved the vast majority of the world's countries forming two opposing military alliances: the Allies and the Axis powers.
The Declaration of Independence was adopted by the Second Continental Congress meeting in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, on July 4, 1776.
The Industrial Revolution was the transition to new manufacturing processes in Europe and the United States, in the period from about 1760 to sometime between 1820 and 1840.
