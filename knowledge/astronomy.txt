Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System. It is a gas giant with a mass more than two and a half times that of all the other planets in the Solar System combined.
Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in the Solar System, after Jupiter. It is known for its prominent ring system.
Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and the second-smallest planet in the Solar System. It is often referred to as the "Red Planet" due to the effect of the iron oxide prevalent on its surface.
Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It is named after the Roman goddess of love and beauty. As the brightest natural object in Earth's night sky after the Moon, Venus can cast shadows.
